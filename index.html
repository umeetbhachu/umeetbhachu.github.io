About Me

Behind the Papers

Umeet Bhachu is a Professional Engineer registered in the Province of Alberta, specializing in rotating equipment, turbomachinery, machinery reliability, and asset performance. His work focuses on practical engineering leadership for pumps, compressors, gas turbines, mechanical seals, lubrication systems, condition monitoring, and reliability improvement across operating energy assets.

He currently serves as a Staff Rotating Equipment Engineer with Pembina Pipeline Corporation, providing technical leadership, engineering oversight, and reliability support for critical rotating equipment across major pipeline and midstream assets.

Prior to Pembina, he worked with Canadian Natural Resources Limited in a corporate technical authority role for turbomachinery and rotating equipment reliability. His experience includes technical standards, troubleshooting, engineering governance, project support, commissioning, and complex machinery problem solving across major industrial assets.

In addition to traditional rotating equipment engineering, Umeet has developed advanced capabilities in data analytics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence, with an interest in applying these tools to machinery diagnostics, condition monitoring, performance optimization, and reliability decision-making.
