Independent development. UBHRA is offered as an independently developed, general-purpose engineering software tool based on established Dynamic Vibration Absorber and beam-theory principles. It is not presented as affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any employer, plant operator, OEM, or other organization.
Engineering and beta-release notice. UBHRA is a preliminary engineering design, screening and field-assistance tool. Calculated frequencies, dimensions, stresses, placement concepts and tuning positions are engineering estimates and starting points. Actual performance depends on the machine, structural response, mounting stiffness, fabrication tolerances, boundary conditions, operating conditions and field installation. Final fabrication and installation should be verified with qualified engineering judgement, appropriate vibration measurements and applicable safety requirements. UBHRA v0.4.4 is currently distributed as an unsigned Windows beta release, so Windows may display an unknown-publisher or Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warning during installation.
