What it does

From service bulletin PDFs to an evidence-backed engineering action register

Reliability and maintenance teams may need to review large numbers of technical and service bulletins and determine which actions apply to which machines. Engineering Compliance Studio is designed to help structure that process while keeping the original source document central to every decision.

  • Reads user-supplied bulletin PDFs and extracts key engineering information such as affected equipment, model and serial-number criteria, required actions, intervals, parts, manpower and closure requirements.
  • Retains supporting evidence by associating extracted information with source quotation and page-reference information so the user can verify the result against the original document.
  • Builds structured action cards that summarize the engineering issue, applicability information and required follow-up for each bulletin.
  • Screens equipment applicability using entered model, serial-number and configuration information. Results can include Applicable, Applicable – configuration review required, Not applicable or Engineering review required.
  • Routes uncertainty to engineering review. When available evidence is insufficient for a reliable determination, the program is designed to return Engineering review required rather than force an applicability conclusion.
  • Exports an Excel engineering compliance register containing structured action information, equipment applicability, supporting evidence, document references and exceptions for follow-up.
  • Supports Ask-the-PDF engineering questions using local RAG. Users can ask questions in plain language and review answers together with the supporting source-document context.
Evidence control

Designed to keep the source document central

Source-grounded retrieval

Engineering Compliance Studio is designed to retrieve supporting evidence from user-supplied technical documents and present source quotation and page-reference information for human review.

The software is intended to support engineering review rather than replace the original bulletin, manual, drawing or governing document.

Human-Verified Compliance

Where available evidence is insufficient for a reliable applicability determination, the workflow intentionally supports an Engineering review required outcome.

Final engineering, maintenance, compliance, operational and safety decisions remain with the qualified user.

Important limits

Designed to support engineering review, not replace it

  • Every result must be verified against the original source document. A screening result should not be treated as an independent engineering conclusion.
  • “Engineering review required” is an intentional safe outcome. It may appear frequently where bulletin wording, configuration information or applicability evidence is incomplete or ambiguous.
  • PDF quality matters. Scanned, image-based, unusually formatted or complex documents may not be extracted completely or consistently.
  • Applicability validation is currently focused on LM6000 bulletin examples. Other turbine families and engineering documents may be processed for evidence retrieval, but their applicability workflow has not been similarly validated.
  • AI-generated wording may vary. Source evidence, page references and the original technical document should remain the basis for engineering decisions.
Local processing

User-supplied documents remain central to the workflow

Engineering Compliance Studio uses a locally hosted AI model through Ollama. Document analysis is performed locally on the user's computer, and the application is not designed to send supplied PDF documents to a cloud AI service.

How AI is used: The local language model is used to identify and organize candidate engineering evidence from the supplied documents. Applicability logic and engineering screening rules are implemented separately in the application, with uncertain cases routed to engineering review.

Users supply their own authorized technical documents. No OEM technical documents are distributed with the application.
